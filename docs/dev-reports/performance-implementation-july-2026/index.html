ELI5: Learning Moments

1) Faster walk: The players were taking small slow steps. We told them to move their legs faster, so they reach places with more energy.

2) Less repeated math: The camera was redoing a heavy math update every frame even when nothing changed. Now it only does that when needed.

3) Reuse map setup: If you build the same Lego base 100 times, that is wasteful. We now save that base and reuse it when the shape is the same.

4) Why this matters: Less repeated work means smoother animation and less battery/CPU use, especially on smaller devices.

5) Connector caching idea: Instead of re-searching the whole bracket every redraw, we keep a quick address book of important nodes and reuse it until layout/view changes.

6) Resize throttling idea: When the window keeps resizing, we wait for the next paint frame and do one position update, not dozens of repeated geometry reads.

7) Budget tests idea: We wrote a checklist robot that inspects hot paths and fails if expensive patterns come back, so performance stays protected over time.
